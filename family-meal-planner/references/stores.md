# Store profiles

A store profile tells you what a store is good for, when its prices change, and what a shopper needs to get the advertised price. Write one profile per store the family uses, in their `household.md` or a separate stores file.

## Profile template

```
### <Store name> — <location>
- Role: core | planned stops only | deals only
- Best for: <categories>
- Store brand: <name>
- Membership or loyalty: <none | card | paid membership>; sale prices need it? <yes/no>
- Digital coupons: <yes/no — clip in app before shopping>
- Ad cycle: <weekday to weekday>; preview posted <day>
- Deal types: <weekly ad, BOGO, multi-buy, rollback, instant savings, none>
- Online: <pickup / delivery / none>; saved or reorder lists? <yes/no>
- Weekly ad URL: <url>
- Notes: <anything that changes prices or trips>
```

## Common US chains: starting notes

Ad cycles vary by region. Confirm the day from the store's own ad page before relying on it.

| Chain | Typical pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Walmart | No weekly ad; Rollbacks and clearance; store brand Great Value; pickup is free and saved lists make reordering quick |
| Sam's Club | Membership; monthly Instant Savings rather than a weekly ad; store brand Member's Mark; Scan & Go app |
| Costco | Membership; monthly coupon book; store brand Kirkland Signature |
| Aldi | Low everyday prices on mostly store brands; weekly ad and "Aldi Finds"; online prices via delivery apps run higher than in store |
| Trader Joe's | Fixed prices, no sales, no online ordering; plan as a list for a specific stop |
| Kroger family (incl. Harris Teeter) | Loyalty card needed for sale prices; digital coupons; weekly ads often reset Wednesday |
| Albertsons family (incl. Safeway) | Club card needed for sale prices; digital coupons; weekly ads |
| Ahold Delhaize (Giant, Stop & Shop, Food Lion) | Loyalty card prices; digital coupons; Giant's ads typically run Friday to Thursday, Food Lion's Wednesday to Tuesday |
| Publix | BOGO deals that often beat everyday prices |
| H-E-B | Combo deals and strong store brands |
| Wegmans | Everyday low prices plus weekly sale items; strong store brand |

## Adding any store

1. Find the store's weekly ad page and note its start day and preview day.
2. Identify the store brand by looking up a staple (milk, bread).
3. Check whether sale prices need a loyalty card or clipped coupons.
4. Check online options (pickup, delivery, saved lists).
5. Fill in the profile and confirm the role with the family.

## Picking core stores

Most families save the most with two or three core stores:

- a warehouse club for bulk proteins, paper goods, cleaners and long-lasting staples;
- a discount grocer for produce, dairy and weekly items;
- optionally one conventional supermarket for items the others don't carry and for its meat sales.

Every other store is used only for planned stops or when a deal clears the worth-the-drive threshold.
