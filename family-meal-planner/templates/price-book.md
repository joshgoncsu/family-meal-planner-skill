# Price book

*Prices actually paid, one row per priced line from receipts. Never record card digits, loyalty numbers, names or addresses.*

*Size, unit price and unit are filled in only when the receipt shows them. Regular price is the shelf price before any sale or loyalty savings. Deal names the discount type: loyalty card, digital coupon, store sale, rollback, instant savings, or none.*
